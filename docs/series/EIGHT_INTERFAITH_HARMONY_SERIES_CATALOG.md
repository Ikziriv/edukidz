### Series 01: The Global Friendship Garden / Petualangan Taman Sahabat Dunia
- **Series ID:** `series-01-friendship-garden`
- **Target Age:** Ages 6–8 (Primary Explorers)
- **Primary Setting:** An urban community courtyard with raised garden beds, rain-catchment barrels, and a shared gazebo.
- **Pedagogical Core:** Growth mindset, caring for living things, discovering that just as diverse seeds produce a lush garden, friends of diverse faiths and backgrounds make a community vibrant.

#### Character Roster & Clothing Dossier
1. **Aisyah Rahma (Indonesian / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Pastel-mint sport hijab with breathable stretch cotton, casual indigo denim jacket over a white tee, beige joggers, teal sneakers.
   - *Role:* Natural project coordinator and botany lover; records seedling germination in her journal.
2. **Mateo Silva (Brazilian / Christian):**
   - *Attire:* Ochre zip-hoodie with rolled-up sleeves, relaxed navy jeans, red canvas sneakers, soft curly hair.
   - *Role:* Musical and animal lover; constructs compost bins and plays soothing guitar chords to calm garden bumblebees.
3. **Kenji Takahashi (Japanese / Buddhist & Shinto Heritage):**
   - *Attire:* Long-sleeve striped navy shirt, olive cargo shorts, modern round tortoiseshell glasses, slip-on shoes.
   - *Role:* Architectural tinkerer; constructs gravity-fed bamboo water canals.
4. **Amara Okafor (Nigerian / Interfaith Family):**
   - *Attire:* Sunflower-yellow cotton tunic with subtle geometric Ankara trim, turquoise leggings, neatly braided hair with wooden beads.
   - *Role:* Athletic motivator; leads soil tilling and encourages teammates when storms threaten seedlings.

#### 1-Week (7-Day) Episodic Chapter Manifest (Days 1–7)
1. **Day 1 (Monday / Senin) — The Empty Lot & Aisyah's Dream (Lahan Kosong & Impian Aisyah):**
   - *Plot:* Aisyah brings her grandmother's heirloom ginger seeds and invites Mateo, Sofia, Mei-Ling, Kenji, and Amara to turn an overgrown lot into a shared garden.
2. **Day 2 (Tuesday / Selasa) — Planting & Rainwater Furrows (Menyemai Benih & Parit Air Hujan):**
   - *Plot:* Mateo digs neat furrows with his trowel while Kenji mists the seeds using a recycled rainwater nozzle (*Gotong Royong*).
3. **Day 3 (Wednesday / Rabu) — Amara's Compost & The First Sprouts (Kompos Amara & Tunas Hijau Pertama):**
   - *Plot:* Amara enriches the soil with organic compost as the first emerald shoots emerge; the friends share *kue lapis* and *pão de queijo*.
4. **Day 4 (Thursday / Kamis) — Sofia's Honeybees & Golden Sunflowers (Lebah Madu Sofia & Bunga Matahari Emas):**
   - *Plot:* Sofia sketches blooming sunflowers in her Nordic knit hijab while observing honeybees carrying golden pollen across blossoms.
5. **Day 5 (Friday / Jumat) — Kenji's Bamboo Canal & Peaceful Rest (Saluran Bambu Kenji & Jeda Siang Damai):**
   - *Plot:* Kenji builds a gravity-fed bamboo water channel from the stone well. When afternoon prayer time arrives, the friends pause peacefully in the shade in mutual respect.
6. **Day 6 (Saturday / Sabtu) — Mei-Ling's Harvest & Shared Baskets (Panen Raya Mei-Ling & Keranjang Berbagi):**
   - *Plot:* Mei-Ling and Amara harvest ripe strawberries, ginger rhizomes, and sunflowers into woven baskets and brew warm ginger tea for neighborhood elders.
7. **Day 7 (Sunday / Minggu) — Starlight Lantern Festival of One Week (Festival Lentera Bintang Akhir Pekan):**
   - *Plot:* All six friends hang glowing cultural lanterns (*Damar Kurung*, Japanese paper lanterns, Chinese silk lanterns, Nordic stars) around the thriving garden, celebrating one full week of unity in diversity.

### Series 02: The Starlight Observators / Penjaga Bintang Semesta
- **Series ID:** `series-02-starlight-observators`
- **Target Age:** Ages 6–8 (Primary Explorers)
- **Primary Setting:** Global mountaintop and treehouse observatories linked by a magical shortwave radio network.
- **Pedagogical Core:** Astronomical science, navigation history, recognizing that all humanity lives under one shared sky.

#### Character Roster & Clothing Dossier
1. **Nurul Izzah (Indonesian / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Midnight-navy hijab printed with subtle golden constellations, cozy fleece jacket, dark grey slacks, trekking shoes.
   - *Role:* Stellar cartographer; maps southern constellations (*Pari/Crux*) and lunar cycles.
2. **Tariq Al-Mansoor (Moroccan / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Warm camel-colored fleece zip-up, canvas cargo pants, breathable desert sneakers.
   - *Role:* Astrolabe and ancient optics enthusiast; demonstrates how ancient scholars created star charts.
3. **Liam MacLeod (Scottish / Christian/Celtic):**
   - *Attire:* Forest-green hooded windbreaker, blue denim jeans, sturdy mud boots, cheerful freckled face.
   - *Role:* Weather observer and cloud watcher; measures wind currents and humidity.
4. **Mei-Ling Chen (Chinese-Singaporean / Taoist & Buddhist Heritage):**
   - *Attire:* Puffer vest over a lilac long-sleeve tee, sporty leggings, violet sneakers, hair tied in two neat buns.
   - *Role:* Optical lens engineer; polishes telescope mirrors and records lunar craters.

#### Episodic Story Manifest (3 Episodes)
1. **Episode 1: The Same Moon for Everyone (Satu Rembulan untuk Kita Semua):**
   - *Plot:* Nurul in Yogyakarta and Liam in Edinburgh look at the moon at the same time through their connected scopes. They realize that distance does not divide people whose hearts are curious.
2. **Episode 2: The Constellation Map (Peta Rasi Bintang):**
   - *Plot:* Tariq shares stories of how desert travelers used stars to find water, while Nurul explains how Indonesian Bugis sailors used stars to cross oceans.
   - *Tolerance Lesson:* Wisdom belongs to all cultures; diverse peoples have guided each other across history with respect.
3. **Episode 3: The Midnight Comet of Peace (Komet Perdamaian Tengah Malam):**
   - *Plot:* A rare green comet drifts across the horizon. The children invite their families to view it together from the hill.
   - *Tri-Modal Adaptations:*
     - *Study Mode:* Tap planets in 3D to learn distance, orbital speed, and bilingual terms (*Gravity / Gravitasi*, *Constellation / Rasi Bintang*).
     - *Sleeping Mode:* Deep indigo sky fading to warm candlelight; binaural space-harp sleep tones.
     - *Playing Mode:* Connect-the-stars 3D constellation drawing sandbox.

### Series 03: The Little Bread Bakers of Sunflower Street / Sahabat Dapur Roti Manis
- **Series ID:** `series-03-sunflower-bread-bakers`
- **Target Age:** Ages 3–5 (Early Explorers / Usia Dini)
- **Primary Setting:** The warm, flour-dusted communal kitchen and bakery of Sunflower Street.
- **Pedagogical Core:** Sensory development, sharing food, gratitude, celebrating that every culture kneads dough with love.

#### Character Roster & Clothing Dossier
1. **Fatima Hassan (Egyptian-Canadian / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Cheerful floral headscarf/hijab pinned safely with a soft clip, bright red apron over a simple cream dress, yellow sneakers.
   - *Role:* Master dough kneader; loves rolling pita and flatbreads.
2. **Oliver Smith (British / Christian):**
   - *Attire:* Denim overalls with a big front pocket, striped t-shirt, rubber boots, permanent dab of flour on his nose.
   - *Role:* Yeast and rise watcher; giggles when bread dough bubbles up like a balloon.
3. **I Wayan Danu (Balinese-Indonesian / Hindu):**
   - *Attire:* Modern cotton polo shirt, yellow waist sash (*selendang*), woven shorts, barefoot on the clean tiled kitchen floor.
   - *Role:* Flavor master; brings sweet grated coconut and palm sugar (*gula aren*).
4. **Maya Ramirez (Mexican / Catholic):**
   - *Attire:* Bright turquoise dress with embroidered sunflowers, white sneakers, hair held back with a polka-dot headband.
   - *Role:* Bread decorator; shapes dough into braids, stars, and animal shapes.

#### Episodic Story Manifest (3 Episodes)
1. **Episode 1: The Dough That Rose to the Sky (Adonan Roti yang Mengembang):**
   - *Plot:* The children mix flour, water, and yeast. They watch with wide eyes as the dough grows big and soft like a warm pillow.
2. **Episode 2: Many Shapes, One Oven (Beda Bentuk, Satu Panggangan):**
   - *Plot:* Fatima rolls round Pita, Wayan wraps sweet banana bread in banana leaves, Oliver bakes round loaves, and Maya braids sweet buns. All go into the same warm stone oven!
   - *Tolerance Theme:* Although our traditions look and taste different, they all share warmth, nourishment, and bring happiness to our families.
3. **Episode 3: The Grand Picnic Basket (Keranjang Piknik Gembira):**
   - *Plot:* The children pack baskets and distribute fresh bread to neighborhood grandparents, nurses, and postal workers.
   - *Tri-Modal Adaptations:*
     - *Study Mode:* Big tactile buttons explaining kitchen vocabulary (Yeast / Ragi, Flour / Tepung, Share / Berbagi).
     - *Sleeping Mode:* The soothing scent of baked bread; crackling hearth fire soundscape with soft acoustic guitar lullaby.
     - *Playing Mode:* Tap-and-knead 3D interactive dough simulator with smiling bread characters.

### Series 04: Whispering Rainforest Guardians / Ekspedisi Rimba Lestari
- **Series ID:** `series-04-rainforest-guardians`
- **Target Age:** Ages 9–10 (Junior Thinkers & Environmental Stewards)
- **Primary Setting:** The emerald canopy of tropical river basins (Kalimantan and the Amazon).
- **Pedagogical Core:** Ecology, biodiversity, traditional conservation wisdom, interfaith environmental stewardship (*Eco-Theology*).

#### Character Roster & Clothing Dossier
1. **Siti Nurhaliza (Sumatran-Indonesian / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Technical moisture-wicking khaki outdoor hijab, olive field shirt with multi-pocket vest, trekking pants, durable trail shoes.
   - *Role:* Drone pilot and camera-trap analyst; tracks endangered hornbills and tapirs.
2. **Bagas Dayak (Kalimantan / Christian & Dayak Heritage):**
   - *Attire:* Dark green quick-dry outdoor jersey, woven bead bracelet from his elders, river sandals, field pack.
   - *Role:* Botanical and forest trail tracker; teaches sacred forest conservation laws (*Tana Ulen*).
3. **Leo Yawanawa (Peruvian / Indigenous Amazonian Heritage):**
   - *Attire:* Waterproof shell jacket, sturdy boots, traditional seed necklace honoring river spirits.
   - *Role:* River navigator and acoustic bio-monitor; identifies bird and amphibian calls.
4. **Priya Sharma (Indian / Hindu):**
   - *Attire:* Practical outdoor tunic with cargo trousers, sturdy hiking boots, field microscope in her messenger bag.
   - *Role:* Soil chemist and mycorrhizal network researcher; shows how tree roots communicate underground.

#### Episodic Story Manifest (3 Episodes)
1. **Episode 1: The Singing Canopy (Kanopi yang Bernyanyi):**
   - *Plot:* The team sets up acoustic microphones in the towering ironwood trees to record rainforest health. They discover that a healthy forest has a chorus where every species has its voice.
2. **Episode 2: The Guardians of the Clear River (Penjaga Sungai Jernih):**
   - *Plot:* Silt from illegal clearing upstream threatens the village drinking water. The children unite local elders from Muslim, Christian, and indigenous villages to plant vetiver grass and bamboo to stabilize the riverbanks.
   - *Interfaith Harmony Principle:* Environmental stewardship is a sacred duty shared by all faiths—caring for creation (*Amanah Bumi*) binds all people together.
3. **Episode 3: Seeds for Tomorrow's Century (Benih untuk Seabad ke Depan):**
   - *Plot:* The team builds a community tree nursery, passing rare dipterocarp saplings to younger kids.
   - *Tri-Modal Adaptations:*
     - *Study Mode:* 3D layer inspection of rainforest ecology (Forest Floor, Understory, Canopy, Emergent Layer) with bilingual science facts.
     - *Sleeping Mode:* Gentle tropical rain falling on broad leaves with rhythmic river water sounds.
     - *Playing Mode:* 3D river navigation puzzle avoiding rocks and rescuing stranded river turtles.

### Series 05: The Harmony Orchestra of Many Sounds / Orkestra Nada Semesta
- **Series ID:** `series-05-harmony-orchestra`
- **Target Age:** Ages 6–8 (Primary Explorers)
- **Primary Setting:** An open-air floating pavilion on a tranquil lake surrounded by pine trees.
- **Pedagogical Core:** Music theory, auditory empathy, teamwork, polyphony (how contrasting sounds blend into harmony).

#### Character Roster & Clothing Dossier
1. **Budi Santoso (Sundanese-Indonesian / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Contemporary navy linen shirt with subtle Batik Parang cuff details, relaxed trousers, modern sneakers.
   - *Role:* Plays the bamboo Angklung and Suling flute; understands pentatonic melodies and group sync.
2. **David Rosenberg (Jewish-American / Jewish):**
   - *Attire:* Blue knitted sweater over an open-collar shirt, dark denim pants, sneakers, dark blue velvet kippah held with a clip.
   - *Role:* Violinist; plays joyful and soulful Klezmer melodies and classical scales.
3. **Zoya Karimova (Uzbek / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Soft blush-pink silk headscarf, embroidered modern long vest over a cream long-sleeve tee, comfortable slip-on shoes.
   - *Role:* Dutar (two-string lute) and rhythm maker; creates intricate tempo changes.
4. **Chloe Dubois (French / Interfaith/Secular):**
   - *Attire:* Striped Breton boatneck shirt, mustard cardigan, dark corduroy pants, Chelsea boots.
   - *Role:* Cellist and conductor; balances bass resonance and chord progressions.

#### Episodic Story Manifest (3 Episodes)
1. **Episode 1: The Day the Instruments Disagreed (Hari Ketika Nada Berselisih):**
   - *Plot:* Each child thinks their instrument should play the main solo. When they all play at full volume at the same time, it becomes a loud, chaotic noise that scares away the lake swans!
2. **Episode 2: Listening Before Playing (Mendengar Sebelum Bersuara):**
   - *Plot:* Chloe asks everyone to close their eyes and listen to each other's notes one by one. Budi shakes his angklung; David adds a soft violin counterpoint; Zoya taps the rhythm; Chloe supports with deep cello chords.
   - *Tolerance Lesson:* True harmony does not mean everyone plays the exact same note; it means listening with empathy so everyone's voice has space to shine.
3. **Episode 3: The Lakeside Concert Under the Stars (Konser Tepi Danau):**
   - *Plot:* The children perform their unified symphony for the entire town.
   - *Tri-Modal Adaptations:*
     - *Study Mode:* Interactive sound wave physics, pitch, resonance, and bilingual musical terms (*Rhythm / Irama*, *Harmony / Keselarasan*).
     - *Sleeping Mode:* Soothing 432Hz string and bamboo woodwind lullaby; screen gently dimming with floating musical notes.
     - *Playing Mode:* 3D tactile instrument playground where tapping instruments composes personalized tunes.

### Series 06: Lanterns of Peace Across the Ocean / Lentera Damai Seberang Samudra
- **Series ID:** `series-06-lanterns-of-peace`
- **Target Age:** Ages 3–5 (Early Explorers / Bedtime Sanctuary Specialty)
- **Primary Setting:** A peaceful coastal harbor with wooden boardwalks, gentle fishing boats, and warm lighthouses.
- **Pedagogical Core:** Overcoming fear of the dark, feeling sheltered and loved, mutual protection across communities.

#### Character Roster & Clothing Dossier
1. **Hana Lestari (Indonesian Coastal Village / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Soft pastel-peach cotton hijab, cozy teal rain jacket, bright yellow gumboots.
   - *Role:* Crafts traditional *Damar Kurung* paper lanterns painted with scenes of flying birds and swimming fish.
2. **Arlo Lindgren (Swedish / Christian):**
   - *Attire:* Chunky cable-knit off-white wool sweater, navy trousers, waterproof warm boots, blond hair.
   - *Role:* Carves wooden lantern frames and tends the small harbor beacon.
3. **Ananya Patel (Indian / Hindu):**
   - *Attire:* Saffron-colored cotton tunic with embroidered hem, leggings, soft leather sandals, bright joyful smile.
   - *Role:* Paints decorative terracotta *Diyas* (oil lamps) that float gently on water.
4. **Karim Mansour (Turkish / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Striped sailor sweater, denim overalls, cozy beanie, warm woolen mittens.
   - *Role:* Rings the harbor brass bell with a gentle, calming chime to signal sunset.

#### Episodic Story Manifest (3 Episodes)
1. **Episode 1: When the Evening Mist Rolled In (Saat Kabut Senja Tiba):**
   - *Plot:* A thick, cool sea mist rolls into the harbor as the sun sets. The little boats out at sea need light to find their way home to their families.
2. **Episode 2: Four Lanterns on the Pier (Empat Lentera di Ujung Dermaga):**
   - *Plot:* Hana, Arlo, Ananya, and Karim each bring a lantern from their home. One is made of painted paper, one of carved pine, one of terracotta clay, and one of brass glass. Placed side by side on the pier, their combined light cuts through the mist!
   - *Moral Value:* Every family has a unique light to offer; when we stand together, nobody gets lost in the dark.
3. **Episode 3: Safe Harbor, Peaceful Dreams (Dermaga Aman, Mimpi Tenang):**
   - *Plot:* The boats dock safely. Parents hug their children, and the entire harbor settles into sleepy stillness under the warm amber lanterns.
   - *Tri-Modal Adaptations:*
     - *Study Mode:* Big glowing touch-targets explaining ocean tides, fog, and lighthouse mirrors.
     - *Sleeping Mode:* Ultra-slow water lap soundscape, distant lighthouse pulse, and gentle bedtime whisper narration.
     - *Playing Mode:* Drag-and-drop colorful lanterns along the harbor boardwalk.

### Series 07: The City of Bridges & Open Doors / Kota Seribu Jembatan Sahabat
- **Series ID:** `series-07-city-of-bridges`
- **Target Age:** Ages 9–10 (Junior Thinkers & Civic Builders)
- **Primary Setting:** A thriving historic and modern riverside city where quarters with different architectural heritages face each other across canals.
- **Pedagogical Core:** Urban design, civil discourse, conflict resolution, building inclusive public institutions that welcome all.

#### Character Roster & Clothing Dossier
1. **Maryam Al-Idrisi (Moroccan-Dutch / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Forest-green modern jersey hijab, denim utility jacket with sketch pencils in the chest pocket, slim-cut dark trousers, white sneakers.
   - *Role:* Civil architect and structural engineer; specializes in arched stone and sustainable timber bridges.
2. **Ibrahim Malik (Indonesian / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Relaxed charcoal hoodie over a modern batik shirt, grey chinos, skateboarding sneakers.
   - *Role:* Community organizer and mediator; listens to neighborhood elders and translates their needs into civic proposals.
3. **Samuel Tadesse (Ethiopian / Christian):**
   - *Attire:* Red-and-black track jacket, dark joggers, high-top running shoes, energetic and expressive posture.
   - *Role:* Urban surveyor and accessibility champion; ensures all bridges have smooth ramps for wheelchairs, strollers, and bicycles.
4. **Lin Wei-Ting (Taiwanese / Buddhist Heritage):**
   - *Attire:* Crisp white oversized poplin shirt, navy pleated trousers, minimal slip-on shoes, digital stylus pen in hand.
   - *Role:* 3D CAD designer and landscape planner; incorporates rooftop gardens and solar lampposts into the bridges.

#### Episodic Story Manifest (3 Episodes)
1. **Episode 1: The River Between Us (Sungai yang Memisahkan):**
   - *Plot:* An old ferry stops working, cutting off the market quarter from the school and clinic quarter. People from both sides start misunderstanding each other because they no longer talk daily.
2. **Episode 2: Blueprint for the Open Bridge (Rancangan Jembatan Terbuka):**
   - *Plot:* The children host a town forum. Some want a tall modern bridge, others want an ancient stone design. Maryam and Lin draft a design that merges stone arches honoring the historic mosque with modern bamboo curves and solar lamps.
   - *Civic Lesson:* Compromise and inclusive design do not mean sacrificing beauty; they create something stronger that everyone feels proud of.
3. **Episode 3: The Ribbon-Cutting of Friendship (Pita Persahabatan Terbentang):**
   - *Plot:* The bridge opens with a festival where citizens walk across to embrace neighbors, exchange gifts, and establish a permanent shared library on the bridge center.
   - *Tri-Modal Adaptations:*
     - *Study Mode:* Physics of bridge load distribution, tensile strength, and historical bridge architecture across continents.
     - *Sleeping Mode:* Ambient city night soundscape (distant boat horn, soft river ripples, gentle acoustic piano).
     - *Playing Mode:* 3D bridge building simulator where kids test bridge strength using physics-based toy trucks.

### Series 08: The Dream Catcher Caravan / Kafilah Mimpi Damai Bawah Rembulan
- **Series ID:** `series-08-dream-caravan`
- **Target Age:** Ages 3–5 & 6–8 (Sleeping Sanctuary Masterwork)
- **Primary Setting:** A mythical, tranquil twilight caravan that travels along the silk roads, dunes, and Indonesian highlands as dusk settles.
- **Pedagogical Core:** Pediatric sleep hygiene, deep emotional comfort, universal bedtime blessings, calming the nervous system.

#### Character Roster & Clothing Dossier
1. **Leila Bint-Suhail (Bedouin / Muslim):**
   - *Attire:* Flowing deep indigo linen robe with silver embroidery along the sleeves, soft twilight headscarf, leather traveling slippers.
   - *Role:* Caravan navigator; sings traditional Bedouin night poems that comfort camels and travelers under the stars.
2. **Joko Raden (Central Javanese / Muslim & Kejawen Heritage):**
   - *Attire:* Traditional striped *Lurik* cotton nightclothes, comfortable soft cap (*peci halus*), warm wool socks.
   - *Role:* Tea maker; brews calming ginger-lemongrass tea (*Wedang Jahe*) and plays a three-hole bamboo lullaby flute.
3. **Siobhan Kelly (Irish / Christian):**
   - *Attire:* Emerald-green soft fleece sweater, warm leggings, sheepskin booties, red hair loosely braided for bed.
   - *Role:* Story weaver; tells gentle Celtic tales about sleeping hills and resting sheep.
4. **Chen Dorje (Himalayan / Tibetan Buddhist Heritage):**
   - *Attire:* Saffron fleece vest over a cozy grey hoodie, soft felt slippers, carries a small singing bowl.
   - *Role:* Sound therapist; strikes the bronze singing bowl to create long, resonant, soothing vibrations that slow the heart rate.

#### Episodic Story Manifest (3 Episodes)
1. **Episode 1: The Caravan Gathers at Twilight (Kafilah Berkumpul di Batas Senja):**
   - *Plot:* As the orange sun dips behind the hills, the caravan lights four small campfires. The travelers prepare for a long night of peaceful rest.
2. **Episode 2: The Whispered Wishes of the World (Bisikan Doa Anak Negeri):**
   - *Plot:* Children from faraway cities and villages whisper their nighttime prayers into the cool breeze: for their mothers, their grandparents, their pets, and for peace on Earth. The caravan gathers these wishes and turns them into golden fireflies that float into the sky.
   - *Bedtime Lesson:* No matter where you sleep tonight—in a tall apartment, a wooden village house, or a quiet tent—the same peaceful night wraps around you like a warm blanket.
3. **Episode 3: Sleep Now, Little Explorers (Tidurlah, Penjelajah Cilik):**
   - *Plot:* The campfires slowly turn into glowing embers. One by one, the travelers curl up under starry blankets. The narrator's voice slows down to a gentle whisper.
   - *Tri-Modal Adaptations:*
     - *Study Mode:* Gentle facts about nocturnal animals (owls, fireflies, night-blooming flowers).
     - *Sleeping Mode:* Circadian decay engine with exponential volume fade over 20 minutes, terminating in a pure black OLED screen with gentle night-clock.
