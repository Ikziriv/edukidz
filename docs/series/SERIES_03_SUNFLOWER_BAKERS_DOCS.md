## 1. Executive Summary & Educational Intent

### 1.1 Story Premise
Down at the sunny corner of **Sunflower Street (Jalan Bunga Matahari)** sits a warm, flour-dusted communal neighborhood kitchen with a big clay-brick oven that smells of sweet cinnamon and roasted seeds. Four little children—**Fatima** (wearing a cheerful floral hijab and tiny red apron), **Oliver** (in blue denim overalls with flour dusted on his nose), **Wayan** (wearing a soft Balinese yellow waist sash), and **Maya** (wearing a bright sunflower-embroidered dress)—gather every weekend with their families to bake bread together.

For toddlers and preschool children, food is the most concrete, joyful bridge between cultures. While Fatima’s family loves round pocket pita bread, Wayan’s family wraps sweet coconut-banana rice bread in green banana leaves, Oliver’s grandma bakes golden braided loaves, and Maya’s mother bakes shell-shaped sweet *conchas*. When all the breads bake side-by-side in the same warm oven, the whole neighborhood fills with the sweet aroma of friendship and safety.

### 1.2 Learning & Developmental Objectives
1. **Sensory Cognition (Ages 3–5):** Exploring textures (powdery flour, squishy dough, warm baked crust), sounds (bubbling yeast, crackling stone oven), and gentle colors.
2. **Early Food Hospitality:** Experiencing that offering bread and warm food to friends, neighbors, and elders is a universal act of love across every religion and culture.
3. **Gentle Normalization of Modest Attire & Cultural Symbols:** Toddlers see Fatima's floral hijab as cheerful and natural, just like Maya's embroidered dress or Wayan's sash.
4. **Bedtime Security:** Providing warm, comforting reassurance before sleep with images of full tummies, kind smiles, and cozy beds.

## 3. Episodic Story Manifest

### Episode 1: The Dough That Rose to the Sky / Adonan Roti yang Mengembang
- **Episode ID:** `story-03-01-dough-rising`
- **Sensory Experience:** Warm water, sweet honey, and dancing yeast. The children tap the bowl and watch the dough double in size like a gentle sleeping cloud.
- **Bilingual Phonics Focus:** *Flour / Tepung*, *Yeast / Ragi*, *Rise / Mengembang*, *Warm / Hangat*.
- **Tri-Modal Adaptations:**
  - *Study Mode:* Big bouncy buttons showing what yeast eats to grow; audio pronunciation with joyful sound effects.
  - *Sleeping Mode:* The dough rests peacefully under a clean checkered cloth; soothing acoustic cradle melody.
  - *Playing Mode:* Tap-to-knead 3D dough simulator where dough giggles and squishes under fingers.

### Episode 2: Many Shapes, One Warm Oven / Beda Bentuk, Satu Panggangan
- **Episode ID:** `story-03-02-one-oven`
- **Story Arc:** Fatima shapes round pita disks, Oliver makes round buns, Wayan wraps sweet sticky banana bread in banana leaves, and Maya braids sweet egg buns. They all slide into the grand stone oven together!
- **Tolerance Lesson:** Just like different shapes of bread bake happily in the same oven, our families have different traditions that all bring love and sweet warmth to our street.
- **Tri-Modal Adaptations:**
  - *Study Mode:* Matching game pairing each bread shape with its name and family tradition.
  - *Sleeping Mode:* The soft amber glow of the stone oven coals, gentle hearth crackle soundscape, 0.75x soothing voice.
  - *Playing Mode:* Drag-and-drop colorful dough toppings (sesame seeds, berries, honey glaze).

### Episode 3: The Grand Neighborhood Picnic Basket / Keranjang Piknik Gembira
- **Episode ID:** `story-03-03-picnic-basket`
- **Story Arc:** The children pack four wicker baskets with fresh bread. They walk down Sunflower Street, handing warm loaves to the postman, the kindergarten nurse, and neighborhood elders sitting in the sunshine.
- **Moral Value:** The joy of sharing (*Berbagi*). Giving food to others makes our own hearts full of happiness.
- **Tri-Modal Adaptations:**
  - *Study Mode:* Vocabulary of feelings (*Generous / Dermawan*, *Joy / Gembira*, *Gratitude / Bersyukur*).
  - *Sleeping Mode:* Sun setting over Sunflower Street; tired children cuddling in their soft beds; gentle lullaby.
  - *Playing Mode:* Interactive delivery bike where kids steer a tricycle across a cartoon neighborhood.

## 5. Parent & Educator Guide (Ages 3–5)

### 5.1 Simple Daily Connection Prompts
- **EN:** *"Look at Fatima's floral headscarf. Isn't it pretty? What colors do you see? What clothes do you like to wear when we bake together?"*
- **ID:** *"Lihat jilbab bermotif bunga milik Fatima. Indah sekali ya? Warna apa saja yang kamu lihat? Pakaian apa yang kamu sukai saat kita membuat kue bersama?"*

### 5.2 Hands-On Tactile Activity
- Try making simple salt dough or baking cookies at home after reading. Let the child shape round balls and discuss how sharing treats makes friends happy.

### 5.3 Bedtime Affirmation
- **EN:** *"Tonight we are safe, warm, and loved. Thank you for our food, thank you for our family, and thank you for our friends. Sweet dreams!"*
- **ID:** *"Malam ini kita merasa aman, hangat, dan disayangi. Terima kasih atas makanan hari ini, terima kasih atas keluarga kita, dan terima kasih atas sahabat-sahabat kita. Selamat tidur nyenyak!"*
