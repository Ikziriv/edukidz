## 1. Educational Premise & 2-Week (14-Day) Narrative Philosophy

### 1.1 Story Premise
In a vibrant, sunlit neighborhood called **Harmony Valley (Lembah Harmoni)**, six children from diverse cultural and religious backgrounds come together across **two full weeks (14 days: Week 1 & Week 2)**:
- **Week 1 (Days 1–7):** Transforming an empty community courtyard into a blooming 9-plot Friendship Garden culminating in a Sunday Lantern Festival.
- **Week 2 (Days 8–14):** Expanding their curiosity through **Classroom Smartboard & Encyclopedia Study**, **Peaceful Muslim Prayer (*Wudhu*, *Sajadah*, *Shalat* & *Du'a*)**, and **Visiting the Harmony Sanctuary Zoo** to care for gentle herbivores (baby giraffes, spotted deer, and bunnies).

### 1.2 Core Developmental Objectives
1. **2-Week Habit & Rhythm Scaffolding:** By structuring the story into **14 Daily Chapters (`Day 1 / Hari 1` to `Day 14 / Hari 14`)** with Week & Activity Filter Tabs, children experience a complete cycle of nature stewardship, classroom study, wildlife empathy, and spiritual gratitude.
2. **Authentic Muslim Prayer & Interfaith Harmony:** Children learn the beauty of *Wudhu* (washing hands, face, arms, head, and feet with pure water), unfolding the *Sajadah* facing the *Qibla*, the 5 daily prayers following the sun's journey (*Subuh, Dzuhur, Ashar, Maghrib, Isya*), and whispering *Du'a* & *Alhamdulillah*—while friends of all backgrounds stand in warm, respectful solidarity.
3. **Bilingual Vocabulary & Science Building:** Practice tier-2 words in both English and Indonesian across botany, zoology (*Sanctuary, giraffe, herbivores, habitat*), and Islamic & universal values (*wudhu, sajadah, shalat, du'a, Alhamdulillah, peace*) paired with interactive *"I Wonder Why?"* science cutaway diagrams (`zoo-habitat`, `prayer-peace`, `seed-roots`, `water-cycle`, `sun-photosynthesis`, `sound-waves`).

## 5. Pedagogical Questions for Parents & Educators

1. **Before Story Time (Diskusi Awal):**
   - *EN:* "Look at the 7-Day Week Calendar! Which day of the week is your favorite, and which friend leads that chapter?"
   - *ID:* "Lihat Kalender Cerita 7 Hari (1 Minggu) ini! Hari apa yang paling kamu sukai, dan siapa sahabat yang memimpin bab tersebut?"
2. **During Interactive Exploration (Eksplorasi Aktif):**
   - *EN:* "Click through Days 1 to 7 on the Chapter Rail or click 'Next Day' on the Farm HUD—watch how the 9 garden plots grow from tiny seeds on Monday to a full harvest on Weekend!"
   - *ID:* "Klik Hari 1 sampai Hari 7 pada Kalender Bab atau tombol 'Hari Baru'—perhatikan bagaimana 9 petak kebun tumbuh dari benih pada hari Senin hingga panen raya di akhir pekan!"
3. **Bedtime Reflection (Renungan Menjelang Tidur):**
   - *EN:* "On Day 7, all six friends lit lanterns under one starry sky. What kind thing did you do this week?"
   - *ID:* "Pada Hari ke-7, keenam sahabat menyalakan lentera di bawah satu langit berbintang. Kebaikan apa yang telah kamu lakukan minggu ini?"
